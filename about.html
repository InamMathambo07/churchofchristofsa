Our Motto:

Christ is the head of the church - Ukrestu yintloko yebandla.

Our Vision:

"Our goal is to build the body of Christ His church by the power of His holy spirit with his word so we can be ready for His return."

Our Mission:

"Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you."
