Youth Conference Postponed

Due to a district gathering that took place in Gqeberha in May, many of our church members incurred additional expenses while attending the event. As a result, a number of members were unable to secure sufficient funds to attend the Youth Conference that was scheduled to take place in Alice. After careful consideration, the Youth Conference has therefore been postponed until next year. The conference will be held in Alice, the same location originally planned. We look forward to coming together next year for a meaningful time of fellowship, spiritual growth, and encouragement among our youth. We appreciate everyone's understanding and look forward to a successful conference next year.
